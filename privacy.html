Last updated: 6 October 2026

What this application does

Rebels Nmbrs MCP is an internal tool. It connects to the Nmbrs REST API on behalf of a signed-in Nmbrs user and lets an AI assistant read payroll and HR information from Nmbrs, such as companies, employees, salary, leave balances and payroll runs. The application only reads data. It does not create, change or delete anything in Nmbrs.

Who can use it

Only people within our own organisation who hold a Nmbrs account with access to the relevant debtor. Each session works with a single debtor.

Data we access

When a user signs in through Nmbrs, they grant the application read access to the scopes listed on the consent screen: company information, payroll settings, leave, employee information, organisational structure, payment (salary) data and employment (contract) data. We do not request access to bank account details or write permissions. Citizen service numbers (BSN) are removed from results by default.

Data we store

The application does not keep a database of employee or payroll data. The only data it stores is the OAuth access token and refresh token issued by Nmbrs, kept in a token file readable only by the account that runs the application. Tokens are removed when the user deletes that file or revokes the application in Nmbrs.

How data is used and shared

Data retrieved from Nmbrs is returned to the AI assistant that the user is talking to, so it can answer the user's question. This means the data is processed by the provider of that assistant under the terms the user's organisation has agreed with that provider. We do not sell data and do not share it with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Logging

Technical logs may record the time of a request, the name of the tool that was called and whether it succeeded. Logs do not contain citizen service numbers, bank account numbers or salary amounts.

Legal basis and retention

Processing takes place on behalf of the organisation that owns the Nmbrs data, for its own payroll and HR administration. We keep tokens only for as long as the application is in use. Retention of the underlying payroll and HR data remains governed by Nmbrs and the data owner.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (AVG) you have the right to access, correct and erase personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Because the underlying data lives in Nmbrs, requests are handled together with the organisation that owns the Nmbrs environment. Send requests to merijn.kersten@rebels.io. You can also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
